The Compliance Data team owns the foundational data layer that every regulatory obligation at Coinbase depends on. We are a centralized team within Coinbase's Platform organization, and we build the trusted, certified data that the rest of the company relies on to answer regulators with confidence.

At the center of our work is the Compliance Data Mart (CDM), Coinbase's canonical source of truth for user, transaction, and compliance data. Our focus is the data foundations beneath every regulatory report, making sure they are accurate, consistent, well-modeled, and trustworthy.
